The tricky part is how many different failures produce the same symptom. Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Keep an eye out for the following clues that it's time to bring in an expert:

HVAC Repair Prevention Tips

A little routine maintenance goes a long way toward avoiding a mid-season breakdown. Put these technician-approved steps to work around your property:

  • During heat waves and cold snaps, set the thermostat a few degrees closer to the outdoor temperature to ease the load
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of grass, leaves, and shrubs so the condenser coil can breathe
  • Clear the condensate drain every spring so algae never backs it up and shuts the system down
  • Leave every supply and return vent open and free of furniture or rugs
  • Change the filter every one to three months so the blower and coil never starve for air
  • Schedule professional maintenance twice a year, before cooling season and before heating season
  • Take new noises seriously; a squealing motor bearing is far cheaper before it seizes
